Can Dogs Eat Strawberries? Yes — Wash and Limit

Yes, strawberries are safe for dogs in moderation. They are low in calories and full of vitamin C, but their natural sugar means they should stay an occasional treat.

ADVERTISEMENT

Are strawberries good for dogs?

Fresh strawberries bring vitamin C, fiber, and antioxidants with very few calories — a good sweet treat for dogs watching their weight.

How to serve them

  • Wash well and hull them. Slice for small dogs.
  • A few berries for a small dog; a small handful for a large one.
  • Offer them plain — no syrup, whipped cream, or sugar.
Skip strawberry-flavored products and desserts; the added sugar and xylitol risk are not worth it. A mashed berry can also be frozen for a cooling chew.

When to skip

Diabetic dogs should avoid the extra sugar. Stop if your dog gets an upset stomach after eating them.

Frequently asked questions

How many strawberries can a dog eat?
A few for small dogs, a small handful for large dogs, and only as an occasional treat.
Can dogs eat frozen strawberries?
Yes — plain, washed, frozen berries make a cooling summer treat.
Are strawberry tops safe for dogs?
The green tops are not toxic, but most dogs prefer the fruit; remove them if your dog is a picky eater.

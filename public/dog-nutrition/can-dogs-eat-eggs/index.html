Can Dogs Eat Eggs? Cooked, Plain, and Moderate

Yes, cooked eggs are a healthy, protein-rich addition to a dog's diet. Raw eggs are best avoided because of salmonella risk and a biotin concern.

Are eggs good for dogs?

Cooked eggs are packed with protein, fatty acids, and vitamins. Scrambled or boiled plain, they are an easy way to add nutrition for a picky or recovering dog.

How to serve them

  • Boil or scramble with no oil, butter, salt, or seasoning.
  • One egg a few times a week is plenty for most dogs; adjust by size.
  • Let cooked eggs cool before serving.
Avoid raw eggs. Raw egg white contains avidin, which can interfere with biotin (a B vitamin) over time, and raw eggs carry a salmonella risk for both dog and handler.

When to skip

Dogs with pancreatitis or a known egg sensitivity should avoid them. Introduce slowly if eggs are new.

Frequently asked questions

Can dogs eat scrambled eggs?
Yes — plain scrambled (no butter, salt, or oil) is a healthy treat or topper.
Why not raw eggs for dogs?
Raw egg white can interfere with biotin, and raw eggs may carry salmonella.
How many eggs can a dog eat?
About one egg a few times per week for most dogs, adjusted for their size and diet.

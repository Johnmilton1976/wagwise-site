Can Dogs Eat Pumpkin? Yes — Plain, Cooked, and Helpful

Yes — plain cooked pumpkin (never the sugary pie filling) is one of the most useful foods to add to a dog's bowl. Its fiber helps firm up loose stool and ease mild constipation.

Is pumpkin good for dogs?

Plain pumpkin is one of the few "people foods" vets routinely suggest for dogs. It is low in calories and rich in soluble fiber, which soaks up excess water in the gut (helping firm up loose stool) and adds gentle bulk (helping mild constipation). That two-way effect is why a spoonful of plain pumpkin is a common first step for a dog with a slightly off tummy — though it is a helper, not a cure for anything serious.

Pumpkin nutrition at a glance

For scale, here is what is in about one cup of cooked pumpkin (245 g):

NutrientAmount per cup
Calories~49 kcal
Protein1.8 g
Fiber2.7 g
Vitamin AAbout 245% of a person's daily value
PotassiumNotable amount

Vitamin A is the headline here — pumpkins are among the richest natural sources — but a balanced dog food already covers a dog's needs, so pumpkin is a topper, not a vitamin fix.

How much pumpkin for a dog?

Start small and build up. A useful daily amount mixed into meals:

Dog weightPlain pumpkin per day
Under 5 kg1/2–1 teaspoon
5–15 kg1–2 teaspoons
15–30 kg1–2 tablespoons
30 kg and up2–3 tablespoons
Too much fiber backfires — pile on the pumpkin and you can trade loose stool for gas, bloating, or the other extreme. A little goes a long way.

Plain pumpkin vs pumpkin pie filling

Never use pumpkin pie filling. It is sweetened and spiced, and the spice mix often includes nutmeg, which contains myristicin and is toxic to dogs. Only ever use 100% plain pumpkin with nothing added.
  • Buy canned "100% pumpkin" (not "pumpkin pie mix") and read the label — the only ingredient should be pumpkin.
  • Or cook fresh pumpkin plain: peel, remove seeds and stringy bits, steam or bake with no salt, sugar, or butter.

Canned or fresh?

Both are fine as long as they are plain. Canned 100% pumpkin is convenient and consistent; fresh cooked pumpkin works too. The deciding factor is the label — if it lists sugar, spices, or "pie," it stays in the human pantry.

How to serve it

  • Stir the measured amount into your dog's regular food.
  • Introduce gradually over a few days so the gut adjusts.
  • For a constipated dog, the same small amount helps add bulk — but if there is no stool for more than a day or two, or any vomiting, see a vet.

What about pumpkin seeds?

Plain, roasted, unsalted pumpkin seeds are not toxic and some owners feed a few ground-up seeds. They contain compounds studied for gently loosening some intestinal parasites, but that is not a substitute for proper veterinary deworming. Keep seeds plain — no salt, no oil, no spice — and only a small sprinkle.

Can puppies eat pumpkin?

Yes, a tiny amount of plain pumpkin once a puppy is weaned and eating solid food can help with mild tummy upset. Keep portions very small and watch the stool.

What if my dog ate pumpkin pie filling?

A small lick is unlikely to cause more than a stomach upset, but pie filling's nutmeg is the concern. A large amount, or any signs like disorientation, rapid heartbeat, tremors, or vomiting, means call a vet or poison control. Plain pumpkin itself is the safe choice; the spiced version is the risk.

When pumpkin isn't enough

Pumpkin is for mild, short-lived gut wobbles. Persistent diarrhea, blood in stool, repeated vomiting, lethargy, or a painful belly are not "add pumpkin" situations — they need a veterinarian. Frequently asked questions

Can dogs eat canned pumpkin?
Yes — as long as it is 100% plain pumpkin with no added sugar or spices. Avoid pumpkin pie filling.
Is pumpkin pie filling safe for dogs?
No — it is sweetened and spiced, and the nutmeg it often contains is toxic to dogs.
How much pumpkin for dog diarrhea?
A small daily amount (1/2 tsp for tiny dogs up to 2–3 tbsp for large) can help firm stool, but see a vet if diarrhea persists beyond a day or two.
Can pumpkin help a constipated dog?
Yes — its fiber adds bulk, but introduce it gradually and consult a vet if there is no stool for more than a day or two.
Can puppies eat pumpkin?
Yes, a tiny amount of plain pumpkin once weaned; keep portions very small and watch the stool.
Are pumpkin seeds OK for dogs?
Plain, roasted, unsalted seeds are not toxic in small amounts, but they are not a replacement for veterinary deworming.
How often can I give my dog pumpkin?
Small amounts can be added daily as a topper, but keep total treats under about 10% of daily calories.
Can diabetic dogs eat pumpkin?
In moderation it can fit a diabetic plan because it is low in calories, but confirm portions with your vet.

How Much Should I Feed My Dog? (By Weight and Age)

Start from your dog's target weight and the food's calorie label, then adjust by body condition — you should feel ribs but not see them.

ADVERTISEMENT

Begin with the label, then adjust

Every bag has a feeding chart by weight. Use it as a starting point, not gospel — activity, age, and metabolism change the real need.

How often to feed

  • Puppies under 4 months: 3-4 small meals a day.
  • Puppies 4-12 months: 2-3 meals.
  • Adults: 2 meals a day is ideal for most.

The body-condition check

You should be able to feel your dog's ribs with light pressure but not see them. From above, there should be a visible waist. No waist = too much food; visible ribs = too little.

Signs to adjust

  • Gaining or losing: shift portions by about 10% and re-check in two weeks.
  • Always measure with a cup — "a handful" drifts fast.
  • Count treats in the daily budget; they add up quietly.

Frequently asked questions

How many cups of food per day for a dog?
It varies by food calorie density and dog size — follow the bag's chart by weight, then adjust by body condition.
How often should I feed my puppy?
Three to four meals a day under four months, dropping to two by adulthood.
How do I know if I feed too much?
Loss of a visible waist and difficulty feeling ribs means too much. Reduce portions about 10% and recheck in two weeks.

Can Dogs Eat Cheese? In Small Amounts, Sometimes

Most dogs can eat a small amount of cheese, but many are lactose intolerant and all should keep it occasional because of the fat and salt.

ADVERTISEMENT

Is cheese safe for dogs?

Cheese is not toxic, and a thin slice is a handy way to hide a pill or reward a dog. The catch is that many dogs lose the ability to digest lactose as they age, and cheese is high in fat and sodium.

How much and which kind

  • Stick to low-sodium, low-fat types: cottage cheese or a small bit of mozzarella are better choices.
  • Keep it to a bite or two — not a snack bowl.
  • Avoid blue cheeses and anything with added herbs, garlic, or onion.
Watch for gas, bloating, or loose stools after cheese. Those are signs of lactose intolerance, and the treat should stop.

When to skip

Overweight dogs and dogs with pancreatitis should avoid cheese. High-fat cheese can trigger a painful pancreatitis flare.

Frequently asked questions

What cheese is safest for dogs?
Low-fat, low-sodium options like cottage cheese or mozzarella, in small amounts.
Can cheese give dogs diarrhea?
Yes — lactose intolerance is common in dogs, and too much fat can upset the gut.
Can I use cheese to hide my dog's pill?
A tiny amount is a common, safe trick, as long as your dog tolerates dairy.

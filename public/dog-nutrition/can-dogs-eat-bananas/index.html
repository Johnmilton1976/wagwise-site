Can Dogs Eat Bananas? Yes — in Moderation

Yes, bananas are safe for dogs in small amounts. They are a healthy treat, but their sugar and fiber mean they should stay an occasional snack, not a daily staple.

ADVERTISEMENT

Are bananas good for dogs?

Bananas offer potassium, vitamin B6, and fiber. They make a fine low-fat treat, especially for dogs that love fruit.

How much is safe

  • Small dogs: a few thin slices.
  • Large dogs: up to half a banana, occasionally.
  • Treats of any kind should be under 10% of daily calories.
Skip the peel — it is hard to digest and can cause a blockage. And go easy on banana-based products with added sugar.

Ways to serve

Fresh slices, mashed into food, or frozen for a cooling chew on hot days. Mash and stuff into a toy for a slow treat.

When to skip

Diabetic dogs or those on a strict weight plan should avoid the extra sugar. Too much banana can also loosen stools because of the fiber.

Frequently asked questions

Can dogs eat banana peels?
No — peels are tough to digest and can cause blockages. Stick to the fruit and discard the peel.
How much banana can a dog have?
A few slices for small dogs, up to half a banana for large dogs, and only as an occasional treat.
Are bananas bad for dogs with diarrhea?
Bananas' fiber can either help or loosen stools. For an upset belly, plain boiled chicken and rice is the safer first choice.

Can Dogs Eat Peanut Butter? Yes — Read the Label First

Yes, plain peanut butter is a great dog treat — but only if it contains no xylitol, which is extremely toxic to dogs. Always read the label.

ADVERTISEMENT

Is peanut butter safe for dogs?

Plain peanut butter (peanuts, maybe salt) is safe and dogs love it. It is high in fat and calories, so it should stay a small, occasional treat.

The one rule that matters: no xylitol

Xylitol, a sweetener in some peanut butters and other "sugar-free" foods, causes a rapid, dangerous drop in blood sugar and liver failure in dogs. A small amount can be fatal. Check the ingredients every time — brands change formulas.
  • Choose a jar with just peanuts (and salt, if any).
  • A teaspoon for a small dog, up to a tablespoon for a large dog, occasionally.

When to skip

Overweight or pancreatitis-prone dogs should avoid it. And never leave a xylitol product within reach.

Frequently asked questions

Is xylitol peanut butter toxic to dogs?
Yes — xylitol is extremely toxic and can be fatal even in small amounts. Check every label.
How much peanut butter can a dog have?
About a teaspoon for small dogs, up to a tablespoon for large dogs, only as an occasional treat.
Can puppies eat peanut butter?
A tiny amount of plain, xylitol-free peanut butter is fine, but keep portions very small for puppies.

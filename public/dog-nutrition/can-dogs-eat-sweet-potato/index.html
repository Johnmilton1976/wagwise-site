Can Dogs Eat Sweet Potato? Yes — Cooked, Plain

Yes — plain cooked sweet potato is one of the better carbohydrate treats you can share. It is packed with fiber and vitamin A, but only once it is cooked and freed of skin, salt, and spices.

Is sweet potato good for dogs?

Plain cooked sweet potato is a genuinely useful carb to share. A serving brings beta-carotene (the plant form of vitamin A), fiber, vitamin C, vitamin B6, and potassium, with almost no fat. That nutrient mix is why sweet potato shows up as the carbohydrate in many sensitive-stomach and limited-ingredient dog foods. It is a step up from plain white potato in nutritional density — though, like any carb, it belongs in the treat budget, not as a free-for-all.

Sweet potato nutrition at a glance

For scale, here is roughly what is in one cup of cooked, mashed sweet potato (about 200 g):

NutrientAmount per cup
Calories~180 kcal
Fiber6.6 g
Vitamin AAbout 214% of a person's daily value
Vitamin C~24% of a person's daily value
PotassiumNotable amount

Vitamin A is the headline — sweet potato is one of the richest natural sources — but a balanced dog food already covers a dog's needs, so think of sweet potato as a topper, not a vitamin fix.

How much sweet potato for a dog?

Sweet potato is more calorie-dense than people expect, so keep it occasional. A sensible portion by body weight:

Dog weightSafe amount (cooked)
Under 5 kg1–2 teaspoons mashed
5–15 kg1–2 tablespoons
15–30 kg2–3 tablespoons
30 kg and upUp to 1/4 cup, occasionally
Sweet potato is not a low-calorie treat despite the "vegetable" label — a cup is around 180 calories. Count it toward the daily treat budget so it does not crowd out balanced food.

How to serve it

  • Steam or bake plain, with no salt, butter, oil, or spices.
  • Mash it or cut it into small soft cubes for easy eating.
  • If keeping the skin on, scrub it very well; many owners simply peel it to be safe.
  • Skip sweet potato fries, sweet potato pie, and candied yams — the oil, sugar, and spices are the problem, not the vegetable.

Never give raw sweet potato

Raw sweet potato is hard to digest and a choking and blockage hazard, especially for a dog that gulps food. It must be fully cooked before it reaches the bowl.

Can puppies eat sweet potato?

Yes — a small amount of plain cooked sweet potato is fine once a puppy is weaned and eating solid food. Their smaller digestive systems mean keep it to a spoonful and watch the stool.

Sweet potato vs white potato

Both are safe once cooked, but they are not equal. Sweet potato wins on fiber and vitamin A. White potato must be cooked too — raw or green potatoes contain solanine, a compound that is toxic to dogs, so never offer those. For the full rundown of what to avoid, see our toxic foods list. If you want a gentle gut soother instead, plain pumpkin does a similar job with even less risk.

Are there risks?

Sweet potato is healthy for most dogs, but three watch-items apply: the natural sugar means diabetic dogs should keep portions tiny and check with a vet; the carb load means overweight or pancreatitis-prone dogs should limit it; and the high fiber means a large serving in one sitting can cause gas or diarrhea. The leafy tops and vines should not be fed.

What if my dog ate too much?

A dog that overloads on sweet potato mostly risks gas, bloating, and loose stools from the fiber and starch — usually mild and self-limiting with water and a bland next meal. Call a vet if there is repeated vomiting, a painful swollen belly, no stool, or signs of pancreatitis (hunched posture, appetite loss). Frequently asked questions

Can dogs eat sweet potato skin?
Scrubbed and fully cooked skin is not toxic, but it is harder to digest — many owners peel it. The key is that the sweet potato is cooked, never raw.
Can dogs eat raw sweet potato?
No — raw sweet potato is hard to digest and a choking or blockage risk. Always cook it first.
How much sweet potato can a dog eat?
A teaspoon or two for small dogs up to about 1/4 cup for large dogs, only as an occasional treat below 10% of daily calories.
Is sweet potato good for diabetic dogs?
It is naturally high in sugar, so portions should be tiny and cleared with your vet before it becomes a regular treat.
Can puppies eat sweet potato?
Yes, a small amount of plain cooked sweet potato once weaned; keep portions tiny and watch for loose stools.
Is sweet potato or white potato better for dogs?
Sweet potato is more nutritious (more fiber and vitamin A). White potato must be cooked too, because raw or green potato contains solanine, which is toxic.
Can dogs eat sweet potato fries?
No — frying adds fat and the salt and spices are unnecessary. Only plain cooked sweet potato belongs in the bowl.
What if my dog ate too much sweet potato?
Usually just gas and diarrhea that pass. Call a vet for repeated vomiting, a painful belly, no stool, or signs of pancreatitis.

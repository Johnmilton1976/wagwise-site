Dog Training Guides — Calm, Positive Methods

Step-by-step dog and puppy training guides: crate training, leash manners, and stopping biting and pulling with positive reinforcement.

GUIDE

How to Crate Train a Puppy — First Night Included

Crate training works best when the crate feels like a den, not a cage. Feed meals inside, reward calm time, and build up to a full night over one to two weeks.

GUIDE

How to Stop Puppy Biting — Without Yelling

Puppy biting is not aggression — it is teething and play. The fix is to redirect to a toy, reward gentle mouths, and make a brief withdrawal when teeth touch skin.

GUIDE

How to Leash Train a Dog That Pulls

Dogs pull because pulling works — it gets them where they want to go. The fix is simple: the walk only continues when the leash is slack.

Training is less about commands and more about teaching your dog what pays off. These guides use positive reinforcement, so your dog works with you instead of against you.

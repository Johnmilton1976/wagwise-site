Dog care resources we trust

This page collects the places we send readers when a question goes beyond our guides, plus the directories that list WagWise. Every link here is one we have used ourselves while researching this site.

Poison control & urgent reference

  • ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center — 24/7 poison emergency hotline (a consultation fee applies) plus a free searchable list of toxic and non-toxic plants, foods, and household products.
  • Pet Poison Helpline — 24/7 animal poison control for North America, with free, clearly written articles on specific toxins like xylitol, chocolate, and grapes.

Veterinary references behind our guides

  • Merck Veterinary Manual — the veterinary profession's standard reference, free online. Where our dose and symptom guides come from.
  • American Veterinary Medical Association (AVMA) — position statements and owner guides, including the size-adjusted ageing approximation our dog age calculator uses.
  • AKC Expert Advice — breed-specific care and training guidance from the American Kennel Club.

Directories that list WagWise

  • PuppySites.com — one of the oldest hand-maintained directories of dog websites on the web, running since 1999.

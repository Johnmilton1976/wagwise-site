Cat Care Guides — Litter, Scratching & Spraying

Straightforward cat care guides for real household problems: litter box training, stopping furniture scratching, and how to stop a cat spraying indoors.

GUIDE

Cat Litter Training — Faster Than You Think

Cats are naturally clean and most use a litter box with almost no teaching. The work is in the setup: the right box, the right litter, and a good location.

GUIDE

How to Stop a Cat Scratching Furniture

Scratching is normal cat behavior you cannot train away — but you can redirect it. Give appealing posts and make the furniture boring.

GUIDE

How to Stop a Cat Spraying Indoors

Spraying is your cat marking territory with urine on vertical surfaces — different from litter accidents. The fix is reducing stress and conflict, not punishment.

Cats are not small dogs. Their behavior usually has a reason — stress, territory, or a medical issue — and the fix starts with understanding it.

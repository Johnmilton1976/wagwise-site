Contact WagWise Pet

The fastest way to reach us is email: contact@wagwise.org. We read everything and reply within two business days, usually sooner.

What to write to us about

  • Corrections. If a guide contradicts the source it cites, or a rate has changed, tell us. Substantive corrections are reflected in the check date on that guide.
  • Guide requests. Tell us the question you could not find an answer to. Most of our topics started as reader questions.
  • Feedback. If a guide was unclear, too long, or missed the point, that is exactly what we want to hear.

What we cannot do

We are writers, not veterinarians. We cannot diagnose your pet, review X-rays, or advise on medication — for anything urgent or medical, please contact a licensed vet. Everything on this site is general information, not veterinary advice.

For product and partnership enquiries

Use the same address. We test what we recommend and say so when a link is an affiliate link; we do not publish paid placements disguised as guides.

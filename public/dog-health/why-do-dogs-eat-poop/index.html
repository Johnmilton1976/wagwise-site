Why Do Dogs Eat Poop (Coprophagia) and How to Stop It

Eating poop is gross but common, especially in puppies. It is usually behavioral — boredom, exploration, or copying the mother — and rarely a medical emergency.

ADVERTISEMENT

Why dogs do it

  • Puppies explore with their mouths and often outgrow it.
  • Boredom or attention-seeking in under-stimulated dogs.
  • Mimicry of a mother dog cleaning the den.
  • Rarely, a diet lacking nutrients or a malabsorption issue.

Health check first

If it starts suddenly in an adult dog, a vet can rule out parasites or digestive problems. Most cases, though, are behavioral.

Clean up promptly — the single most effective step. A yard with no accessible stool removes the opportunity entirely.

Practical fixes

  • Pick up stool immediately, every time.
  • Teach "leave it" and reward for ignoring it.
  • Add exercise and puzzle toys to cut boredom.
  • Some owners use a taste-aversion supplement after a vet's okay.

Frequently asked questions

Is eating poop dangerous for dogs?
It is usually not toxic, but it can spread parasites and stomach bugs. Prompt cleanup and deworming on schedule reduce the risk.
Will my puppy grow out of eating poop?
Most puppies outgrow coprophagia by a year old once they are better stimulated and the habit is not reinforced.
What supplement stops dogs eating poop?
Taste-aversion products exist; ask your vet first. The reliable fix is cleanup plus training, not a pill alone.

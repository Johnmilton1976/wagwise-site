Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This page covers what this website processes, why, and what control you have. It is written to be read rather than survived.

The short version

WagWise Pet has no accounts, no newsletter and no comment box, so there is very little to collect. The calculators on this site — chocolate toxicity, feeding amounts, age conversion and the poison-dose tool — run entirely inside your browser. The weight, dose and food figures you type are never sent to us, never logged and never stored. Closing the tab erases them.

What is left is advertising and analytics cookies, and those are set only if you agree to them. Nothing optional runs before you choose.

Cookies and consent

On your first visit you are asked to choose. Reject optional is one click and sits next to Accept all rather than buried in a settings menu. Refusing works exactly like accepting: every guide, calculator and image still loads. Before you choose, advertising and analytics storage default to denied, so no such cookie is set at all.

To change or withdraw your choice at any time, use the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page. If you accept, that consent is passed to advertising partners in the standard Google Consent Mode format.

Advertising

Advertising on this site, when it runs, is served by Google. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites, and to measure how those ads perform. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info, or read how Google uses data from sites like this one at policies.google.com. Opting out of personalised ads does not remove ads; it makes them less relevant.

Two house rules matter more to us than an impression count: no pop-ups and no interstitials that cover the guide you came to read, and no ad unit placed in the middle of a poisoning first-aid answer. If you ever see one there, it is a mistake — write to us and it gets pulled.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled after consent, they are aggregate and privacy-respecting: how many people read a guide, and roughly which country they are in. They are not used to identify individuals, not sold, and not combined with advertising data by us.

Affiliate links

Some pages link to retailers. If you buy through one of those links we may receive a commission. It never changes the price you pay, and it does not decide what we recommend — commission rates are not a ranking input, and a product that pays us nothing can still be the pick.

Medical disclaimer

All content is general information, not veterinary advice. For diagnosis, treatment, or anything urgent, contact a licensed veterinarian. In an emergency, call your nearest animal clinic.

A calculator cannot examine an animal. The dose tools compare a number you type against published thresholds; they cannot tell you whether a pet is already symptomatic, and a figure below the threshold is not a reason to wait if the pet is unwell.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of personal data, to receive it in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time. Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents may also ask what personal information is held, request its deletion or correction, and opt out of its sale or sharing — we do not sell personal information.

Because we do not collect personal data through the guides or the calculators, most such requests concern cookies set by advertising partners, and those are controlled through the opt-out links above. Anything else can go to contact@wagwise.org. EU and UK residents may also complain to their local data protection authority.

Children

This site is written for adults caring for a pet and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children; if you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version appears on this page with a new date. If a change affects what is collected or who receives it, it is noted at the top of the page rather than quietly edited in.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about how your data is handled, can be sent to contact@wagwise.org. WagWise Pet is an independent editorial resource and is not affiliated with any veterinary practice, clinic chain or product manufacturer.

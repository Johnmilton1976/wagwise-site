Best Dog Crate 2026 — Practical Picks, Not Hype

The best crate is the one your dog accepts and you can clean easily. We prefer sturdy wire crates with a divider for puppies and a secure plastic crate for travel.

Best Dog Harness for Pulling — Front-Clip Picks

A front-clip harness turns a pulling dog back toward you, which gently discourages leaning in. We favor padded, adjustable, easy-on designs over trendy ones.

We do not test every product on the market. These are the crates and harnesses we would buy for our own dogs, with the trade-offs spelled out.
